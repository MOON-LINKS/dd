# 🏮 My Heart, My Lantern

### ✨ A 3D Interactive Love Experience with AR Finale  
A birthday gift experience built with Three.js

---

## 💖 Description

A heartfelt 3D web experience built with Three.js that takes the user on a cinematic emotional journey.

It begins with a scroll-driven story inside a magical lantern, transitions into an interactive 3D lobby filled with personal interactions, and ends with an AR experience where a crown appears in the real world, along with her nickname and a photo capture moment.

The goal is to create a memorable, emotional, and immersive digital gift.

---

## 🌙 Features / Flow Overview

### 1. 🏮 Scroll Cinematic Journey

- A glowing lantern appears in a dark scene
- Scroll gradually zooms the camera toward it
- The lantern grows and fills the screen
- User enters inside the lantern
- Inside: floating green particles and soft magical lighting
- Text appears:
  > "All souls I only see one"
- Continued scroll reveals:
  - a Violet Purple light
  - Her nickname
  - A cinematic 3D title at top:
    > "My Heart, My Lantern"

---

### 2. 🏠 Interactive Lobby

After the scroll story:

- Transition into a 3D interactive lobby scene
- Contains 3 main elements:
  - “Me” character
  - “Her” character
  - Multiple “+” interaction nodes

#### Interactions:

- Clicking **Me**
  → 3D text: “HII CUTIE 💖”

- Clicking **Her**
  → heart particles / emotional animation

- Clicking **+ nodes**
  → unique romantic messages appear:
  - “You don’t need friends, you have each other”
  - “You are my safe place”
  - “You are my favorite person”

- Top center UI button:
  → “HAPPY BDAY”

---

### 3. 👑 AR Crown Finale

When clicking “HAPPY BDAY”:

- Switch to AR mode (WebXR)
- A golden magical crown appears in real space
- Sparkles and particles surround it
- Her nickname appears under the crown
- User can adjust position by moving phone
- Button appears:
  → 📸 Take Photo

Result:
- Saves a memorable AR snapshot

## 🧰 Technical Requirements

### Engine / Library
- Three.js (r150+)
- Cannon-es (optional physics)
- GSAP (recommended for scroll animations)

### Languages
- JavaScript (ES6+)
- HTML5
- CSS3

### Rendering
- WebGL 2.0

### Interactions
- Raycasting (click detection)
- Scroll-based animation system
- State machine (scroll → lobby → AR)

### AR / XR
- WebXR (AR mode support)

### Devices
- Laptop (Lenovo LOQ usage of camera and aspect ratio)
- plus: Desktop and Mobile (more professional)
